# 🏢 Maskan PMS - Property Management System

**Maskan PMS** is a comprehensive web-based Property Management System designed for Bangladeshi real estate developers, building managers, and shareholders. It streamlines the entire property management lifecycle from construction to operation.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 1. Complete Financial Management
Track every expense and collection in real-time with full transparency.

### 2. Shareholder Management
Manage unlimited shareholders with individual dashboards and ownership tracking.

### 3. Smart Bill Generation
Generate all monthly bills (Electricity, Water, Security, Lift, Maintenance) in under 60 seconds.

### 4. Apartment & Maintenance Control
Manage all apartments, maintenance fees, and utility distribution from one place.

### 5. Multi-Project Support
Manage multiple buildings and projects from a single dashboard.

---

## 📋 Key Modules

- **Project Management** - Create and manage multiple building projects
- **Shareholder Management** - Ownership tracking, share transfers, credit balance
- **Apartment Management** - Unit details, maintenance fees, assignment
- **Expense Management** - Invoice tracking, document upload, supplier management
- **Payment Management** - Payment collection, allocation, discount handling
- **Billing System** - Monthly bill generation, utility distribution
- **Subscription Management** - Per-apartment pricing, flexible duration, trial period
- **Reporting** - Financial reports, shareholder ledgers, expense summaries
- **Multi-User Roles** - Manager, Accountant, Shareholder with role-based access
